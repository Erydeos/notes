# **The 36 Best Blender Tutorials for Beginners – Summary**

## **Overview**

The video presents a structured learning path consisting of 36 recommended YouTube tutorials covering the major disciplines of Blender.

The recommendations follow the **80/20 principle**: focus on the relatively small set of skills that provide the greatest practical value.

Each major discipline is divided into three levels:

- **Level 1 – Beginner:** Learn fundamental tools and concepts.
- **Level 2 – Intermediate:** Develop more comprehensive workflows and techniques.
- **Level 3 – Advanced beginner / Early intermediate:** Apply the skills to more complex projects.

The goal is to develop a broad understanding of Blender before choosing a specialization.

## **1. Modeling**

10 tutorials

Focus: Learn Blender's modeling tools, hard-surface modeling, and how to approach models artistically.

### **Level 1 – Fundamentals**

- **Five Blender Models in 7 Minutes – Polygon Runway**
  - Introduction to circular modeling and basic modifier functions.
- **Learn Blender Hard Surface Modeling in 5 Minutes – Polygon Runway**
  - Introduction to hard-surface modeling.
- **Bicycle Modeling Tutorial – Polygon Runway**
  - Apply the previously learned modeling fundamentals in a complete project.

### **Level 2 – Developing Techniques**

- **Hard Surface Modeling Theory – Josh Gambrell**
  - Fundamental modeling concepts.
  - The 70/30 rule: knowing when to add detail and when to keep things simple.
- **Become a Boolean Pro in Blender – Josh Gambrell**
  - Practical Boolean workflows and hard-surface modeling techniques.
- **CG Boost – Modeling Strategies**
  - Useful modeling tools and strategies that improve efficiency.

### **Level 3 – More Complex Modeling**

\- **Robotic Design – Curtis Holt**

- Mini-course covering the modeling of two robotic arms.
- Explores the difference between designing for form over function and function over form.
- Teaches how modeling decisions depend on the intended result.

## **2. Texturing**

8 tutorials

Focus: Learn UV-based and procedural texturing, material creation, realism, and surface imperfections.

### **Level 1 – Fundamentals**

- **Blender Texturing for Beginners – Blender Guru**
  - Nearly two-hour mini-course covering the complete texturing process.
  - Recommended as the most valuable single texturing tutorial.
- **Procedural Worn Metal – Ryan King Art**
  - Create a worn, painted metal material using procedural nodes.
- **Product Texturing in Blender – Video Creator**
  - Complete product-texturing workflow.
  - Includes a practice model and 6K textures.

### **Level 2 – Realistic Materials**

- **CG Boost – Image Textures with Depth**
  - Create detailed image textures for large environments.
  - Use image-based detail instead of modeling everything, saving computing resources.
- **Realistic Procedural Materials – Jamie Dunbar**
  - Create realistic procedural materials by introducing surface imperfections.
- **Edge Masks in Blender – Riley B3D**
  - Learn to create edge masks, which are useful for adding wear and surface detail.

### **Level 3 – Advanced Texturing**

- **Edge Wear and Grunge – Max Hay**
  - Apply edge masks to create realistic edge wear and grunge.
- **Procedural Mapping and Edge Wear – Ryan King Art**
  - Integrate mapping, edge wear, and imperfections into a procedural workflow.

**Important concept:** Surface imperfections are essential for realism. Perfectly clean and uniform materials often look artificial.

## **Main Takeaways**

1. **Follow a structured progression:** Work through beginner, intermediate, and more advanced tutorials rather than jumping randomly between videos.
2. **Build broad knowledge first:** Learn the major Blender disciplines before committing to a specialization.
3. **Understand the reasoning:** Pay attention to why techniques are used, not just which buttons to press.
4. **Explore the creators:** The recommended channels contain additional tutorials and resources beyond the specific videos listed.
5. **Practice through projects:** Apply techniques in actual modeling, texturing, and animation projects.
6. **Specialize based on interest:** Use the initial learning process to discover which areas of 3D art you enjoy most.

**Overall recommendation:** Treat the list as a learning roadmap rather than a checklist. The objective is to develop a solid foundation across Blender's major disciplines, giving you the skills to pursue a specialization later.
